# Atypical Hodge locus is not NL vanishing

Author: Benjamin Stanley Frohman
Copyright (c) 2026 Benjamin Stanley Frohman
License: Apache-2.0

Two different theorems. This file does not title either of them
"Hodge is false." Two Clay-counterexample repos would be false.

## Shelf A — integral Hodge, not rational Hodge

Atiyah–Hirzebruch torsion and Kollár degree phenomena are integral
counterexamples to an integral form of Hodge. They do not disprove
the rational Hodge conjecture that Clay states.

## Shelf B — atypical locus versus vanishing on a very general fibre

Vanishing of extra rational Hodge classes on a very general fibre is
monodromy / Deligne partie fixe. In this setting that vanishing is
already available for d ≥ 3. It is not Baldi–Klingler–Ullmo.

Baldi–Klingler–Ullmo, Invent. Math. 2023, arXiv:2107.08838,
Corollary 1.6: if n = 4 and d ≥ 6 then the primitive VHS of smooth
hypersurfaces in P^{n+1} has level at least 3, and therefore the
positive-period-dimension Hodge locus HL(U_{n,d}, V^{otimes})_{pos}
is algebraic. Level ≥ 3 also forces the typical Hodge locus empty:
every positive-dimensional Hodge component is atypical.

A special subvariety Z is atypical when

    codim(Φ(Z)) < codim(Φ(S)) + codim(Γ_Z \ D_Z).

Plane-containing sextics are the standard example that the expected-
codimension count is not sharp (Griffiths). The locked host

    F = x0^5 x3 + x3^6 + x1^5 x4 + x4^6 + x2^5 x5 + x5^6

with planes Π and Π_{-1} is one point on that example. F_mem_plane
and F_mem_plane_minus1 prove the planes lie on V(F). That is an
instance on shelf B. It is not a proof of Corollary 1.6 and it is
not Clay.
