# Derived categories versus the locked host F

Author: Benjamin Stanley Frohman (@BenFrohman). Apache-2.0.
Not a CycleSection. Not imported by Hodge.lean. Not Clay.

Derived categories of fourfolds are most interesting when the canonical bundle
is trivial or close to it. Then Bondal–Orlov reconstruction fails and D^b(X)
can match another variety (or a noncommutative one) without X being isomorphic
to it.

## Cubic fourfolds

Kuznetsov:

    D^b(X) = ⟨A_X, O_X, O_X(1), O_X(2)⟩
    A_X = ⟨O, O(1), O(2)⟩^⊥

A_X is 2-Calabi–Yau (Serre = [2]), K3-like. For Pfaffian cubics and some plane
cubics he related A_X to D^b(S) or a twisted D^b(S, α). Addington–Thomas matched
this with Hassett’s Hodge-theoretic associated K3; Bayer–Lahoz–Macrì–Nuer–Perry–Stellari made the equivalence complete on admissible C_d.

A plane on a cubic (d = 8) is special for Hodge but not an associated-K3 case
(4 does not divide 8). The derived category still sees the plane as mutation /
projection data; it does not turn A_X into ordinary D^b(S).

Rationality conjecture: X rational iff A_X ≃ D^b(S). Known rational cubics fit;
no cubic is proved irrational.

## Other fourfolds

For S^{[2]}, D^b(S^{[2]}) is controlled by the K3 Mukai lattice (derived Torelli).
Fourier–Mukai partners exist. The Hodge input is Lefschetz (1,1) on the K3,
not (2,2) on a general fourfold.

Calabi–Yau fourfolds have K_X ≃ O, so Bondal–Orlov does not reconstruct X from
D^b(X). Hodge numbers h^{1,1}, h^{2,1}, h^{3,1}, h^{2,2} are large; there is no
Kuznetsov K3 component of cubic type in general. Derived equivalences, if any,
are Fourier–Mukai kernels on X × Y.

D^b(P^2 × P^2) is generated by a Beilinson exceptional collection. That is a
product of projective spaces, not the locked host. A general complete-intersection
fourfold does not have that collection.

## Kernels versus cycle class

An exact equivalence is Fourier–Mukai, with kernel E ∈ D^b(X × Y). If E = O_Z
for a single subvariety, that kernel is an algebraic correspondence. In general
E is a complex, not a surface. Having D^b(X) does not write I(Z_i) from a Hodge
class on X.

## The locked sextic

    F = x0^5 x3 + x3^6 + x1^5 x4 + x4^6 + x2^5 x5 + x5^6

Degree 6, K_X ≃ O, h^{3,1} = 426. There is no splitting

    D^b(X) = ⟨A_X, O, O(1), O(2)⟩

with A_X ≃ D^b(S). The plane Π ⊂ V(F) is the object O_Π in D^b(X), and its
class is [Π]. That is the same algebraic cycle already in F_HOST / SpecialSextic.
Derived categories of fourfolds organize cubics and some hyperkähler fourfolds;
they leave this Noether–Lefschetz sextic as a plane on a Calabi–Yau hypersurface.
