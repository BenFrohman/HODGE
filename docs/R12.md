# dim R_{12}(F)

Author: Benjamin Stanley Frohman  
License: Apache-2.0

Locked host

$$
F=W(x_0,x_3)+W(x_1,x_4)+W(x_2,x_5),\qquad W=u^5v+v^6.
$$

The projective hypersurface is smooth, so the six partials form a regular sequence of quintics and

$$
R(F)\;\simeq\;R(W)^{\otimes 3},\qquad
H_R(t)=(1+t+t^2+t^3+t^4)^6,\qquad
H_R(1)=5^6=15625.
$$

Hilbert function of one atom \(R(W)\):

| deg | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| dim | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 4 | 3 | 2 | 1 |

Triple convolution at the Griffiths slots:

| piece | dim | Hodge type |
|---|---|---|
| \(R_0\) | 1 | \(H^{4,0}\) |
| \(R_6\) | 426 | \(H^{3,1}\) |
| \(R_{12}\) | **1751** | \(H^{2,2}_{\mathrm{prim}}\) |
| \(R_{18}\) | 426 | \(H^{1,3}\) |
| \(R_{24}\) | 1 | \(H^{0,4}\) |

So

$$
\dim R_{12}(F)=1751=h^{2,2}_{\mathrm{prim}}(V(F)).
$$

Total \(h^{2,2}=1751+1=1752\) after the Lefschetz class \(h^2\). These are the same Hodge *numbers* as a general smooth sextic fourfold. Smooth hypersurfaces of fixed degree in \(\mathbb{P}^5\) have constant Hodge numbers. What can jump on an NL locus is the rank of the *rational* lattice \(H^{2,2}\cap H^4(\mathbb{Q})\), not \(\dim R_{12}\).

This count is not a list of surfaces and not \(\gamma_{\mathrm{bad}}\).
