See docs/HDG2_NOT_H2.md and docs/H_NOT_P.md.

    A: ∀X ∀γ ∈ Hdg^2(X), γ ∈ im(cl_X)
    B: ∃X ∃γ ∈ Hdg^2(X), γ ∉ im(cl_X)

They are a statement and its negation. Exactly one is true in mathematics.
A missing term of B is not a proof of A.
Supplying a geometric term of B proves A false.
Supplying a term of A proves B false.
Neither term is supplied. The rational Hodge conjecture is open.

Lefschetz (1,1) is CH^1 → H^2 and is a theorem. It does not inhabit B
and does not block B in codimension 2.
