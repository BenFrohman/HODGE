# Derived Torelli (lock against F)

Author: Benjamin Stanley Frohman (@BenFrohman).
Literature statements. Not a CycleSection. Not Clay. Not a new theorem.

## Classical Torelli (K3)

Two complex projective K3 surfaces are isomorphic iff there is a Hodge isometry
H^2(X,Z) ≡ H^2(Y,Z).

## Derived Torelli (Mukai–Orlov)

D^b(X) ≃ D^b(Y) iff there is a Hodge isometry of Mukai lattices
H̃(X,Z) = H^0 ⊕ H^2 ⊕ H^4 ≈ H̃(Y,Z),
with H̃^{2,0} = H^{2,0}. Huybrechts / Macrì–Stellari: orientation of a
positive 4-plane. Equivalent: transcendental lattices T(X) ≈ T(Y).

Hodge input is Lefschetz (1,1) plus H^{2,0} on a surface. Not Hdg^2 of a fourfold.

## Bondal–Orlov

±K ample ⇒ D^b determines X. Off when K ≈ O (K3, CY fourfolds, F).

## Locked host F

F is a Calabi–Yau sextic in P^5, h^{3,1}=426, not K3^{[2]} type.
Derived Torelli does not write I(Π) from a Hodge class. Π is algebraic by
F_mem_plane; O_Π is that cycle as a sheaf.
