# Open frontiers

The following statements are recorded and remain uninhabited.

## BenFrohman/HODGE

`HodgeConjecture.general_fourfold` for an unspecified geometric sextic fourfold
`V(F) ⊂ ℙ⁵`. The named spans, the `zeroCycle` sentinel, the combinatorial
Hodge numbers, and the rank-nullity rephrasing are proved. The geometric
inclusion is not.

## BenFrohman/CLEMENS

Finiteness of singular rational curves of degree 12 and of all birational
rational curves of degree ≥ 13 on a general quintic threefold. The Cotterill
range, the Ballico–Fontanari smooth degree-12 case, and the universal
normal-bundle Euler-characteristic identities are recorded. The tail is not.
