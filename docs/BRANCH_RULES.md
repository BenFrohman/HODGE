- Default branch is `main`.
- Work happens on a topic branch off `main`. Merge by pull request.
- Every Lean file carries the Apache-2.0 copyright header and the author line
  Benjamin Stanley Frohman (@BenFrohman).
- No `sorry`, `admit`, or silent `axiom` on `main`.
- Do not commit a claimed term of `general_fourfold` or of Term B.
- Do not write S or [Pi] as Y or as a miss.
- Partner Y and the miss stay named and empty until equations exist.
