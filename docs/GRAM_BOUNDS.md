# Gram bounds, not Hodge

Author: Benjamin Stanley Frohman
Date: 28 September 2026
License: Apache-2.0

On the locked host X = V(F) the three classes h^2, [Pi], [Pi_{-1}] are algebraic of type (2,2). Their 3x3 intersection Gram has nonzero determinant. Hence they are linearly independent over Q.

## What stands

    rho >= 3
    dim im(cl) >= 3

Both lower bounds follow from those three independent algebraic Hodge classes.

## What does not stand

    Z = 0

means Hdg^2(V(F)) subset im(cl), i.e. Hodge holds on this host. A lower bound on both sides does not force equality. That would need an upper bound on rho matching the algebraic span, or an upper bound on im(cl) together with a complete list of Hodge classes. Neither is written.

## Ledger

    rho >= 3
    dim im(cl) >= 3
    Z uncomputed

Not Z = 0. Field 3 empty. Hodge open.
