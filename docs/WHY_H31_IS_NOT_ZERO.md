# Why \(h^{3,1}=0\) for \(d=3,4,5\) is false

**Author:** Benjamin Stanley Frohman  
**Copyright:** © 2026 Benjamin Stanley Frohman  
**License:** Apache-2.0

A circulating table set \(H^{3,1}=0\) for smooth hypersurfaces in \(\mathbb{P}^5\)
of degree 3, 4, and 5. That row is wrong. It confuses two different pieces.

Griffiths residues, degree \(d\) fourfold in \(\mathbb{P}^5\):

\[
H^{4,0}\cong R_{d-6},\qquad
H^{3,1}\cong R_{2d-6}.
\]

\(R_k\) is the degree-\(k\) piece of the Jacobian ring of \(F\).
Negative degree is zero. Degree 0 is the constants, dimension 1.

| \(d\) | \(d-6\) | \(h^{4,0}\) | \(2d-6\) | \(h^{3,1}\) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | −3 | 0 | 0 | **1** |
| 4 | −2 | 0 | 2 | **21** |
| 5 | −1 | 0 | 4 | **120** |
| 6 | 0 | **1** | 6 | **426** |

What vanishes for \(d<6\) is \(H^{4,0}\), because \(d-6<0\).
What turns on at \(d=6\) is a holomorphic 4-form, \(h^{4,0}=1\).
\(H^{3,1}\) is already nonzero on a cubic: one-dimensional, generated by
the residue of \(\Omega/F^2\) in degree 0 of the Jacobian ring.

The correct middle table is in [HODGE_NUMBERS.md](HODGE_NUMBERS.md).
The two-theorem split is in [D_GE_6.md](D_GE_6.md):
vanishing of extra *rational* classes is \(d\ge 3\);
BKU level \(\ge 3\) is \(d\ge 6\).
